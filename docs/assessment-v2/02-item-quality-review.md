# 2. Item Quality Review — All 24 Version 2 Items

Each proposed Version 2 item was reviewed against four criteria:

- **Clarity:** one concept, a concrete referent, no leading or absolute terms, no jargon.
- **Reading level:** target grade 8 or below for a workforce-wide survey. Grades are Flesch–Kincaid estimates. They are unreliable for single short sentences and are inflated by the unavoidable word "organization's" (five syllables); cognitive interviews should confirm comprehension.
- **Consistency:** referent, tense and vocabulary consistent within and across dimensions.
- **Independence:** no distinctive phrasing shared with known proprietary or published instruments.

## Consistency notes

1. **Referents.** Version 2 keeps the v1 mix of self-referent items (11) and organization-referent items (13), because each item uses the referent that fits its construct. Personal experience (purpose, recognition, understanding) is asked in the first person; organizational practice (collaboration, processes, adaptability) is asked about "here" or "the organization". The research specialist should record the composition model used to aggregate them (document 4).
2. **"Here" and "this organization".** Both refer to the respondent's organization and are used interchangeably. Cognitive interviews should confirm that respondents in large organizations read "here" as the organization rather than their team.
3. **Tense.** All items are present tense, except LE2 ("has set"), which describes an established state.
4. **Keying.** All items remain positively keyed (a documented trade-off; see document 4).

## Independence screening status

The Version 2 wording was written for this review. It was compared against the structure and widely published items of instruments known to the reviewer: the OCAI, the Gallup Q12, published psychological-safety scales, and common commercial workplace-climate surveys. **No shared distinctive phrasing was identified.** Those comparisons relied on recollection, not licensed copies. **Before publication, all 24 Version 2 items must be screened against licensed copies of the relevant instruments** (prelaunch review, recommendation R2), and the result recorded.
