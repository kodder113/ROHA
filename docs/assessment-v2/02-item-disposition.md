**Totals:** 11 retained (6 of them moved to a new dimension; SI3 differs from v1 IA2 only in its first word), 11 revised, 1 combined, 1 replaced; **3 new items** (OC5, EE5, OE5). 11 + 11 + 3 = 25.

**Notes on the combined and replaced items.**
- **SA4 combined into SI2.** SA2 (my work supports objectives) and SA4 (I understand how my contributions affect success) measured almost the same line-of-sight concept. Keeping both in a five-aspect dimension would waste an aspect on redundancy. SI2 keeps the alignment concept; the freed slot is used for the adaptive-innovation aspects.
- **IA3 replaced.** "Taking action to improve processes that are not working" overlapped with process efficiency (OE1) and with support for employee improvement ideas (SI5). Its slot is replaced by content the framework lacked: procedural clarity (OE5), which the brief asked for. The overall item count is balanced by the three new items.

## 2.3 Owner's final edits (September 26, 2026)

| Item | Earlier draft | Approved wording | Source of the change | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EE5 | I can choose my own approach to getting my work done. | **I have appropriate freedom to decide how to accomplish my work.** | Written by the owner | Same construct (autonomy over how work is done). "Appropriate" frames the item as the right degree of freedom rather than the most freedom, which should reduce the desired-state ceiling (document 4). **Screening flag:** "freedom … how … work" is close to common job-design questionnaire autonomy wording; this is now the highest-priority item for licensed screening (document 3, 3.6). |
| SI3 | This organization adjusts quickly when conditions change. | **This organization adapts effectively when circumstances change.** | Owner restored the Version 1 IA2 wording, changing only "The" to "This" | Measures adaptation effectiveness rather than speed. Comparable with v1 IA2 at item level (document 5, 5.4), with the one-word change noted. Estimated reading grade 19.2 (above the grade-12 guideline because of three long words in a short sentence); to be checked in cognitive interviews (document 3, 3.7). |

Both edits are recorded as owner-authored in the authorship record ([prelaunch review, document 7](../prelaunch-review/07-provenance-and-questions.md)).
